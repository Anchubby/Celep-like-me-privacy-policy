1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Services do not collect or store any personally identifiable information ("PII") or user content on our own servers, including, but not limited to:

  • Names, Email addresses, and Phone numbers
  • Account login credentials (user names, passwords, unique user IDs)
  • Uploaded, captured, or processed photos and images
  • Biometric data or facial geometry profiles

Users are not required to create accounts or submit contact information to utilize the core features of the Services.

2. Information Automatically Collected

The Services integrate with Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. AdMob may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID, IDFA)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and network information
  • Application usage, interactions, and ad engagement analytics
  • Telemetry data for fraud detection and ad performance measurement

The Company does not have access to, nor stores, any of the data collected by AdMob. All such data is handled exclusively in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Use of Automatically Collected Data

Data collected via third-party SDKs (such as AdMob) is used solely for:

  • Delivering, customizing, and optimizing advertisements within the Services
  • Improving runtime stability, website performance, and user experience
  • Preventing fraud, identifying invalid traffic, and ensuring system security
  • Compliance with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

The Company does not use the collected data for remarketing beyond the scope required by AdMob.

4. Real-Time Location Information

The Services do not gather, track, or utilize precise, real-time location information of your mobile device.

5. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Technologies

The Services use on-device machine-learning models to provide the celebrity look-alike feature. When You voluntarily take a photo or select one from Your photo library, the Application processes the image locally on Your device to generate a look-alike result. The Application does not upload the image, facial data, or prediction result to our servers or to any third party.

6. Face Data

What Face Data We Access and Process. The Application accesses and processes only the photo voluntarily selected or captured by You for the look-alike feature, which may contain information related to a human face (“Face Data”). The Application does not use ARKit face meshes, facial landmark data, facial geometry profiles, facial templates, or facial data for authentication or identification.

Purpose of Use. Face Data is used solely to run the on-device look-alike analysis and display the resulting celebrity match. Face Data is not used for advertising, marketing, user profiling, analytics, identity verification, authentication, model training, or any unrelated purpose.

Local Processing and Storage. The selected image is resized and processed locally on Your device by the Application’s bundled machine-learning model. We do not upload, remotely store, or maintain Face Data, face photos, facial templates, facial geometry, or prediction results on our servers. The Application does not save Face Data or prediction results to persistent app storage.

Retention and Deletion. Face Data and the prediction result are retained in the Application’s active memory only while needed to display and process the current result. They are cleared from the Application when You retry or replace the photo, or when the application session ends. The operating system or libraries may create temporary local cache files while processing an image or creating a share image; those temporary files are not accessible to us and are managed and removed according to the operating system’s cache-management practices.

Sharing and Third Parties. We do not share, sell, transfer, or disclose Face Data to any third party, including advertising platforms, analytics providers, data brokers, or service providers. Google AdMob does not receive or access Face Data. If You voluntarily use the Application’s Share feature, the Application opens the device’s native share sheet and shares a result image only with the recipient or service that You personally select. That voluntary sharing is initiated and controlled by You, and the selected recipient’s privacy practices apply.

Your Choice and Consent. You choose whether to provide a photo by granting Camera or Photo Library permission and selecting or capturing an image. You may decline or revoke those permissions at any time in Your device settings. If You do not provide a photo, the look-alike feature cannot analyze an image, but no Face Data is accessed or processed.
